## **1. Habit Management**

- **Add New Habit** – name, description, category (e.g., health, learning, work).
- **Custom Frequency** – daily, weekly, specific days, or even “every X days.”
- **Start & End Dates** – so you can track for a fixed challenge period (e.g., 30 days).
- **Reminders/Notifications** – push notifications or simple alert pop-ups.

---

## **2. Tracking System**

- **Daily Check-in** – click to mark habit as done for the day.
- **Multiple Completion Styles**

  - ✅ Checkbox/toggle
  - 📅 Calendar mark
  - 📊 Progress bar

- **Partial Completion** – e.g., 50% progress if goal was 2 hours of reading but you read 1 hour.
- **Streak Counter** – “You’ve done this habit for X days in a row” (very motivating).

---

## **3. Visualization & Analytics**

- **Calendar View** – highlights days you completed the habit.
- **Progress Charts** – weekly/monthly bar chart or line graph.
- **Streak Heatmap** – GitHub-style green squares to show consistency.
- **Success Rate** – % of days you completed your habit.

---

## **4. Motivation & Gamification**

- **Achievements/Badges** – “5 days in a row,” “Perfect Week,” etc.
- **Level System** – gain XP for completing habits.
- **Leaderboard** _(if multi-user)_ – compare with friends. --nope

---

## **5. Extra Smart Features**

- **Habit Notes/Journal** – log how you felt after doing the habit.
- **Auto Skip for Planned Breaks** – so vacation doesn’t break your streak.
- **Dark/Light Theme** – better UI comfort.
- **Offline Support** – works without internet, syncs when online.

---

## **6. Tech Ideas for Implementation**

- **Local Storage** for offline habit data.
- **IndexedDB** or **SQLite** for bigger data sets.
- **Push Notifications** via browser API.
- **Responsive Design** – mobile-first, but works on desktop.
- **Optional Cloud Sync** (Firebase, Supabase).
